=== BACKGROUND ===
This case involves a multi-hop money mule layering network operating across
Jharkhand and Bihar border. Unlike typical SIM-swap cases, victims here were
cheated via fake job offers and investment schemes on WhatsApp/Telegram. No
OTP redirection, no SIM swap confirmed. Victims willingly transferred money
believing it was a registration fee or "demo trade deposit". Funds then moved
through multiple fake accounts to obscure trail.

Case received after Bank of India Jamui flagged suspicious pass-through
activity on 5 accounts on 07-Apr-2023. Cross-referenced with complaints filed
at cybercrime portal by 10 victims across 3 states.
